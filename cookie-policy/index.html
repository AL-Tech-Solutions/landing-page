Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve performance, and provide analytics. This Website uses analytics cookies only after you have given your consent via the cookie banner.

4. Managing Cookies

You can change your choice anytime via Cookie settings in the footer.

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner: When you first visit, you can accept or reject analytics cookies.

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the Website.
